# Code Of Conduct

## Our Pledge

AgentGate contributors should keep the project respectful, practical, and safety-focused.

Expected behavior:

- Be respectful.
- Stay technical.
- Avoid personal attacks.
- Preserve safety boundaries.
- Avoid sharing private data or secrets.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment.
- Publishing private information.
- Encouraging credential theft, exploit instructions, KYC bypass, payment abuse, or unsafe automation.
- Pressuring maintainers to add unsafe flows.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject PRs, or block contributors who violate this code.
